A MOMENT FOR YOU

Relaxation

Short, gentle exercises you can do anywhere.

❋

GUIDED · 1 MINUTE

Paced breathing

Breathe in for 4, hold gently for 4, and breathe out for 6. Stop if you feel light-headed.

GROUNDING · 3 MINUTES

5-4-3-2-1 senses

  1. Notice 5 things you can see.
  2. Notice 4 things you can feel.
  3. Notice 3 things you can hear.
  4. Notice 2 things you can smell.
  5. Notice 1 thing you can taste.

BODY · 5 MINUTES

Release tension

  1. Sit or lie comfortably.
  2. Gently tense your feet for five seconds, then let go.
  3. Move upward: legs, stomach, hands, shoulders, face.
  4. Notice the difference between tension and release.
  5. Skip any area that is painful or injured.

PRIVACY & CARE

Clear about your care

Your conversations, attachments, hydration entries, and appointments are associated with your account and stored in this app's HEALIX database. Messages and any files you attach are shared with the configured AI provider to produce a reply. Doctors can read a conversation only if you share it with an accepted consultation, and you can revoke that at any time. Administrators cannot read your conversations. Avoid names, contact details, or other identifying information in health questions.

HEALIX AI is for general health education. It cannot examine you, diagnose conditions, or replace a qualified health professional. Do not delay urgent care while using this app.
